Introducing the 2026 Toyota Tacoma 4X4 TRD Off-Road Premium Double Cab AT, finished in striking Heritage Blue and built to deliver serious capability with refined everyday comfort. This four-wheel-drive truck is powered by a responsive 2.4-liter turbocharged 4-cylinder gasoline engine paired with an 8-speed automatic transmission, providing confident performance whether you are navigating city streets, tackling rugged trails, or towing with purpose. With estimated fuel economy of 12.4 KM in the city and 11.4 KM on the highway, the Tacoma balances power and practicality for drivers who demand versatility from their truck. The TRD Off-Road Premium trim enhances the Tacoma’s adventurous character with Bilstein shock absorbers, Crawl Control, Multi-Terrain Select, a locking rear differential, and upgraded underbody protection. A part-time 4WD system, front recovery hooks, all-terrain tires, and heavy-duty tow equipment further reinforce its off-road credibility.

The 5-foot bed includes adjustable tie-down cleats, cargo bed tie-downs, a bed power port, and a full-size spare tire, making it ready for work, travel, and weekend recreation. Inside, the Double Cab cabin blends utility and comfort with SofTex seats, heated and ventilated front seats, an 8-way power adjustable driver and passenger seating, dual-zone automatic climate control, and a heated leather-wrapped steering wheel. Premium technology is highlighted by a 14-inch Toyota Multimedia system with Service and Safety Connect, wireless Apple CarPlay and Android Auto compatibility, a 10-speaker JBL audio system with portable Bluetooth speaker, wireless charging, and multiple USB-C ports. Safety and driver assistance are equally impressive, with Toyota Safety Sense 3.0, blind spot monitor with rear cross-traffic alert, lane tracing assist, pre-collision system, dynamic radar cruise control, parking assist with auto braking, and a comprehensive airbag system.
